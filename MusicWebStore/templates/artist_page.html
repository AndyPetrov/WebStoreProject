Biography

Kanye West is an American rapper, record producer, and fashion designer who has become one of the most influential and controversial artists of his generation. Born in Atlanta and raised in Chicago, West first gained recognition as a producer for Roc-A-Fella Records in the early 2000s. He has since released multiple critically acclaimed albums, won numerous Grammy Awards, and expanded his creative vision into fashion, architecture, and other fields.
